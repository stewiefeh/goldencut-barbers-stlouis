The shop

A storefront on Gravois Avenue in Bevo with a navy awning out front and gold-trimmed chairs inside. The chairs are the first thing regulars mention, and the couch by the photo wall is where the wait happens. Most waits don't.

Services

Straightforward pricing, set at the counter. Ask when you walk in or call ahead.

  • Cuts & fadesThe house specialty. Sharp tapers, skin fades, classic scissor work.
  • Beards & lineupsShape-ups and beard work that keep their edge between visits.
  • Kids' cutsPatient with the wiggly ones. Grandkids and grade-schoolers both leave happy.
  • Every textureFades and textured work done right on every hair type.
  • Walk-ins firstNo appointment needed. Short waits, even on picture-day emergencies.
